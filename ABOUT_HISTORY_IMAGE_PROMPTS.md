## Shared Treatment

- Preserve a consistent 4:3 landscape crop.
- Leave natural negative space for overlapping narrative cards.
- Progress from monochrome to faded color to contemporary neutral color.
- Keep grain and material texture visible.
- Export a 2400px master, 1800px WebP, 1800px AVIF, and 900px mobile crop.
